### Topic research (2026-07-07, triangulated)
- **Timeline:** modern vulcanology (Univ. Naples Federico II) reconstructs a
  ~32-hour event with 17 pyroclastic currents. Pliny the Younger saw the
  umbrella cloud ~1pm (eruption began ~noon); pumice rain crushed roofs for
  ~17h; ~7pm first pyroclastic currents; column collapse + the killing surges
  at daybreak next day — during a lull, survivors tried to flee. Sources:
  [Science/AAAS minute-by-minute](https://www.science.org/content/article/minute-minute-account-pompeii-eruption-revealed-agonizing-detail),
  [Pompeii Archaeological Park / Pliny timeline](https://pompeiiarchaeologicalpark.com/timeline-of-terror-the-eruption-of-mount-vesuvius-as-witnessed-by-pliny-the-younger/),
  [Wikipedia overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eruption_of_Mount_Vesuvius_in_79_AD).
- **Date:** most experts now favor 24 Oct–1 Nov 79 AD, not the traditional
  Aug 24 — a good myth-bust beat.
- **Fresh 2024–26 hooks (recency = differentiation):**
  - Regio IX (Aug 2024): woman on a bed with gold/silver/bronze coins + pearl
    earrings, key in hand; young man (15–20) by the exit — trapped by debris.
    [archaeologymag](https://archaeologymag.com/2024/08/remains-of-two-vesuvius-victims-discovered-in-pompeii/)
  - DNA study (Nov 2024) **rewrites victim identities** — assumed
    mother-and-child groupings were wrong (unrelated males) — huge
    "everything you assumed is wrong" beat.
    [archaeologymag](https://archaeologymag.com/2024/11/dna-evidence-rewrites-history-of-pompeii-victims/)
  - House of Thiasus banquet-hall fresco (2025); AI face reconstruction of a
    victim (Apr 2026). [archaeologymag](https://archaeologymag.com/2026/04/ai-reconstructs-face-of-pompeii-victim/)
- **Daily-life texture (the morning-of beats):** 81 pre-cut loaves carbonized
  in one oven; ~80 thermopolia (Roman fast food); 11,000+ graffiti — election
  slogans (1,500+), love notes, insults, restaurant tips. People were eating
  breakfast and opening shops.
  [worldhistoryedu graffiti](https://worldhistoryedu.com/graffiti-posters-and-signs-in-pompeii/).

# Niche: AI tools → creator/video (✅ LOCKED 2026-06-30)

**Positioning:** tool reviews & comparisons ("I tested X so you don't have to") +
automation/workflow how-tos ("make faceless videos with AI") + specific-tool
tutorials. Faceless via screen recordings; narration = the creator's **own voice**
(never TTS). Money: tool **affiliates (primary)** + SaaS sponsors + ads + eventual
own product. See [[../monetization]].

## Why locked (evidence, 2026-06-30 scrape)
- Enormous demand + the richest money environment tested: ElevenLabs 63M,
  Google 22M, Grammarly 25M, Anthropic 1.7M all producing content.
- Head-on "best AI tools" is giant-dominated (~88% of 100k+ view videos from
  100k+ sub channels) — do NOT fight there.
- The creator/video **niche-down** is the most newcomer-winnable lane tested:
  genuine sub-20k breakouts — @esca.creates 13K→194K, @Grow.Advance. 16K→144K,
  @elijahumama 10K→135K, @VishnuSuthar09 10K→102K.

## The load-bearing finding (never re-litigate without new data)
Across freight, HVAC, and storage ([[closed-niches]]), the "forensic explainer
for curious outsiders" format showed **zero repeatable small-channel breakouts** —
giants (Wendover, How Money Works) own that lane. **Every breakout found was
action-oriented** (buyer's guide, review, tutorial, comparison). Action formats
also stack ads + affiliate + sponsors; explainers earn low-intent ads only.

## Comp channels (study for packaging)
- **@GregEditsVideo** (27K, AI/DaVinci editing) — ~113K views/mo, most consistent
  (19% of videos >50k, median 14K) → ~$0.6–1.2k/mo ads. **The model that works.**
- @esca.creates (13K→194K), @elijahumama (10K→135K), @sandyleeai (47K→190K,
  automation), @JosephMartin (46K→204K, tool reviews).
- @Mr.AICASH (32K, make-money lane, ~$1.5–2.8k/mo + course funnel) — grifter-adjacent,
  trust risk; observe, don't copy.
- Cautionary: @AIforUs50Plus — one 199K hit, median ~1K → ~$30/mo. **A single viral
  hit ≠ a viable channel; always read median + % of videos >50k.**
- Adjacent (2026-07-04 scrape): Chronixel 8k→142K proves the "Claude/AI can now edit
  video — honest hands-on" frame is small-channel-winnable; Nate Herk (838k) owns the
  broad frame. Faceless lane re-confirmed: Aftab Khan 58k→492K, zapiwala ai 120k→523K.

## Process rules extracted (Notion §6)
1. Money-first beats interest-first. 2. Action formats are where newcomers win AND
where the money lives. 3. Validate every pick with the gates — an open-looking topic
can be an AI-slop graveyard under one giant. 4. vidIQ outlier multipliers on
micro-channels are meaningless — read absolute views. 5. Reddit shows demand + hooks,
not winnability — verify on YouTube. 6. Niche-down is how newcomers beat giants.

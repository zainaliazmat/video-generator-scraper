# Monetization — the honest model

## Calibrated by real data (2026-06-30 — protected; no false hope)
- $10K/month from ads alone: ~500K–1M monthly views in a high-RPM niche, 1–2+ years
  in; most channels never get there. **Treat $10K as the ceiling, not the target.**
- Proven small channels in OUR locked niche (25–32k subs, ~1yr+) pull **~$0.6–2.8k/mo
  from ads**. Realistic first milestone: **$1–3K/mo within 12–18 months** for a
  well-run channel. $10K needs ~100k+ subs / 300k+ monthly views + the full stack.
- Milestones to track: YPP monetization → first $100/mo → $1k/mo → scale.

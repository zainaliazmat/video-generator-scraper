# Indian business-culture money mindset & slang

For the India finance channel. Pairs with [[haryanvi-hindi-script-style]] and [[india-finance-market]]. Use these as **flavor + credibility anchors** ("the Marwaris built empires doing exactly this"), never as claims about specific people.

## The two mindsets (both = aspirational money framings)

**Marwari** — frugality + financial discipline: minimize waste, tight expense control, and **reinvest profit** rather than spend it. Rooted in arid-Marwar scarcity. "Every penny saved is a penny earned." Modest personal living even when wealthy. (high confidence — IIT Jodhpur "Socialization of Frugality" + multiple sources)

**Gujarati** — **dhandho** (business/trade) as identity and duty. Framing phrases:
- **"धंधो करो" (Dhandho karo)** — "do business" (literal imperative, widely-used sentiment; ref: Shobha Bondre's book *Dhandha: How Gujaratis Do Business*).
- **"धंधो धरम छे" (Dhandho dharma chhe)** — "business is a duty/dharma." (medium confidence — framing phrase, not a fixed proverb)
Same frugality + reinvestment values at the core.

## Culturally-resonant framings for scripts

- "जो सेठ बणना चाहवै सै, वो पहले बचत करै सै" — whoever wants to become a *seth* saves first.
- Frugality as smart, not stingy: "कंजूसी ना, समझदारी सै।"
- The tijori / khata metaphor for a separate emergency account.

## DO NOT USE (refuted — 0-3 votes)

- Proverb **"Ghar ni lakshmi, ghar ma rakho"** — not verified; drop it.
- Any claim that Marwari empires were built **"specifically"** on saving-over-spending — overstated; frame as *a* trait, not *the* cause.

## TEMPLATE (Fading Lore) — why it's OUR blueprint
- **Hook (0–30s):** cold-open montage of five ordinary people mid-life ("a man
  is buying bread… a stray dog is asleep on the warm stones") → dramatic-irony
  hammer at 0:35: "in about 4 hours, every single one of these people is going
  to look up at the mountain… and realize far too late that they were never
  safe." Payoff promise by 1:19. Then the REFRAME that differentiates it from
  a thousand eruption docs: "Not the eruption. **The morning before it.**"
- **Visual system (from keyframes):** three alternating layers —
  1. AI-cinematic photoreal reconstructions, matched shot-for-shot to VO lines
     (the sleeping dog on the stones IS a frame);
  2. desaturated/moody real ruins footage (modern Pompeii as the "evidence"
     layer);
  3. **parchment-texture motion-graphic cards** — myth-vs-reality tables
     ("Gladiator Status: MYTH condemned prisoner / REALITY professional
     athlete"), process diagrams ("The Fiorelli Cast Method 1863", 4 numbered
     steps with illustrations). Motion graphics in period costume — this is
     the HyperFrames-native move.
- **Structure:** hour-by-hour walk through the city's morning (baker, school,
  baths, forum) with Vesuvius recurring as a visual refrain "on the northern
  skyline"; eruption physics land at ~66% ("What I'm about to describe killed
  approximately 2,000 people"); ending is a thesis, not a summary: "a monument
  to an ordinary morning that happened to be the last one… It happened to
  people exactly like you on a morning exactly like this one."
- **Steal:** (1) ordinary-people cold open + dramatic irony; (2) the reframe
  title move ("Not X. The thing before X."); (3) parchment infographic cards;
  (4) second-person direct address at the close.

## FORMAT REF (How So) — the motion-graphics proof
- 41.6x views/sub on pure motion graphics: one continuous textured 3D map,
  animated armies/settlements, a date chip (top-left "850–950 CE"), kinetic
  place labels. 15 titled chapters ≈ 90s each = the compressed-timeline
  rhythm. Zero footage, zero faces — pure craft. Proof the format Channel B's
  pipeline is built for can pull millions.

## Conclusions → our Pompeii script
1. **Open on ordinary life, not the volcano** (template hook, 10.7x proof) —
   five vignettes, dramatic irony, promise by ~1:15.
2. **Three-layer visual system:** AI-cinematic reconstruction (VO-matched) +
   desaturated ruins/evidence footage + parchment motion-graphic cards for
   every myth-bust and process (casts method, eruption physics, DNA rewrite).
3. **Hour-by-hour countdown structure** with Vesuvius as recurring visual
   refrain; eruption physics ≈ 60–70%; fresh 2024–26 discoveries as the ~70%
   payoff beats; close in second person.
4. **Length: 20–32 min** (template 31:37, format ref 22:25 — both breakouts;
   avoid the giant's 70min for video 1).
5. **Title uses the reframe move**, e.g. "Pompeii's Last Day — Not the
   Eruption. The Morning Before It." (final packaging A/B at scripting).

## Round 2 — creator's 3 references (2026-07-08) · packet `research/wj39QiFqCQg/`
Creator supplied 3 more Pompeii videos: a faceless Urdu cinematic, a face
podcast, and a NatGeo doc. Analyzed for style (faceless), facts (NatGeo), and
the religious framing (podcast).

- **FACELESS — "Urdu Cover" (wj39QiFqCQg), ~8 min.** Format = a **"new
  discoveries" listicle**, NOT a day-in-the-life. Hook: "This is the story of
  Pompeii… the city is still speaking to us — every excavation reveals
  astonishing discoveries. Watch till the end." Then discrete discovery beats:
  the bakery + 2,000-yr-old loaves → the **magnificent horse** ("larger than
  ordinary… the royal horse of a wealthy nobleman… found near a grand villa,
  not an ordinary stable") → more. Style: channel-logo watermark, **burned-in
  English captions with keyword highlighting** (red "MOUNT VESUVIUS"), a mix of
  AI/stock Roman footage + real museum photos (the loaves) + historical
  engravings + cinematic landscape footage; moody grade. ⚠️ It says **"89 AD"**
  (wrong — 79) — reference videos carry errors; don't copy them.
  Takeaway: our day-in-the-life frame is more distinctive than this listicle;
  keep ours, but the horse + "still speaking to us" hook are worth borrowing.
- **PODCAST — "Vlog Central" w/ Dr. Nasir Baig (6mnGFC75lWg), ~21 min.** Face
  podcast (RODE mics). This is the **"sins → Allah's punishment" narrative**:
  Pompeii = a "town of sins," lawless "paradise of the self," prostitution
  (men + women), erotic wall art, explicitly compared to **the people of Lut
  (AS)** — the eruption framed as *azab* / a lesson forever. See the honest
  fact-check in [[../../videos/video-hist-01-pompeii/religious-dimension]] —
  it's the popular-preacher reading (overstates the depravity; Pompeii is not
  in Quran/Sunnah). Useful as raw emotional framing, NOT as fact.
- **NATGEO — "Drain the Oceans: Secrets of Pompeii's Dead" (NP4RrJCYFGs),
  44 min.** Fact spine (secular). Confirms: eruption ~midday (Pliny), white→gray
  pumice fall ~1pm–midnight, then column-collapse pyroclastic surges; "boxer
  position" victims (hands to face); doors blocked by pumice = deadly trap;
  those who braved the stone-rain early mostly survived, those who sheltered
  died in the surge. **Civita Giuliana villa**: an entire horse cast + 2 more +
  a gold/silver/bronze chariot in the stable, and beside it a cramped
  **slave-family room** (3 beds, one child-sized — "the invisible people").
  **Herculaneum mystery** (its hook): few bodies in the town, but ~300 died at
  the shore boat-houses waiting for rescue (~10× the town), killed instantly by
  a hotter surge. → strong optional beats: the horse (used), the slave-family
  room, the Herculaneum shore.

## 4. Storyboard (visual + motion + transition, scene by scene)

Reusable grammar for every scene: **photo fills frame → blue‑sepia grade → Ken Burns push (alternating
direction) → text rises in (year, title, caption) → dwell → cross‑dissolve to next.** Per‑scene specifics:

- **S00 Title** — Centered title card over a wide establishing photo (grand railway terminus *or* a
  liner at dock). Slow push‑in. Title fades up large; kicker "1841 — TODAY" above. Hold, then dissolve.
- **S01 1841** — Left layout begins. Victorian crowd / early excursion train. Drift right→left.
- **S02 1869** — Suez Canal / ships in the cut. Drift left→right. *(Optional act‑break: gentle white bloom.)*
- **S03 1883** — Orient Express carriage or platform; rich interior if available. Drift right→left.
- **S04 1897** — Liner departing, dockside crowds, funnels & smoke. Push‑in centered‑ish.
- **S05 1907** — Fast liner at speed / on the Atlantic. Strong horizontal drift (sense of motion).
- **S06 1912** — Towering liner profile in dock; awe, scale. Slow vertical reveal (pan down funnels).
- **S07 1927** — Single aircraft / airfield / pilot. Tilt‑up motion to "look to the sky." *(Optional bloom.)*
- **S08 1936** — Grand staircase / ballroom / Queen Mary. Push‑in on opulence.
- **S09 1939** — Flying boat on water / passengers boarding. Drift left→right.
- **S10 1952** — Sleek jet on the tarmac / in flight. Faster, cleaner push (modernity).
- **S11 1970** — 747 at the gate / crowd of travelers. Wide, optimistic drift.
- **S12 Today** — Modern airport / world map / contemporary travel (color allowed here, or keep graded
  mono for continuity then bloom to a hint of color on the last beat).
- **S13 Outro** — Back to dark. Centered closing line, then **client logo** lockup. Grain + vignette hold.

**Transition default:** ~1.0 s cross‑dissolve (opacity crossfade on overlapping clips). Use a soft
white **bloom** (`css-light`) only at 2–3 act breaks (e.g. into 1869, into 1927) for rhythm.

## The audit's mandatory advisory — hook payoff ≤15s

`audit-hi.md` check 3 made this a build gate: the naming
«नाम है — क्रेडिट रिपोर्ट» must land inside 15s, and required an ffprobe of s1.

h1 measured **17.162s** against a 16.96s estimate — the voice ran **1.2% slower**
than the rate, not the 11.5 c/s worst case the audit feared. The naming clause is
168 of h1's 212 chars (79.2%), so it lands at **≈13.6s of VO, ≈14.0s on the
timeline** with the 0.4s lead-in. **Margin ≈1.0s — the advisory holds, no trim
needed.**

Caveat for the build stage: that 14.0s is a character-offset interpolation, which
script handoff #7 explicitly distrusts for Hindi. Confirm it off the word-level
(faster-whisper) timings when the cues are anchored. If it slips past 15s there,
the audit's prescribed trim is the «पर वो तय करती है कि» clause — never the naming.

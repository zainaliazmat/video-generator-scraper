### 0b. Delivery-score legend — what the marks in the VO mean (read off them at the mic)
| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `/` | short pause — a comma for the ear |
| `//` | full stop — **land it, let the silence sit** |
| `…` | trail off; don't rush the next line |
| `(breath)` | inhale here — at an idea boundary, never mid-sentence |
| **`**word**`** (renders **bold**) | punch it — extra weight + volume |
| `_word_` (renders *italic*) | slow down through this word/phrase |
| `[↑]` / `[↓]` | lift energy / settle down (quieter, intimate) |
| `\|` | beat break — a new idea starts; finish the last one first |

## 3.5 DELIVERY & RECORDING — this is YOUR voice (replaces the old "render the TTS" step)

The narration is a **human performance over screen recordings.** Most delivery problems are written into the script before you record — that's why the marks above exist. Read **off the score**, and:

- **Warm up first** (hum + lip trills) — before the take, not in it.
- **"The mic eats energy."** Push ~**20% past** what feels natural; flat energy is the #1 hook-killer. Match energy to the importance of the line (`[↑]` lines = lift; `[↓]` lines = settle, quieter, intimate).
- **Record beat-by-beat**, not in one perfect take. Reset breath + energy at each `|` beat break. It keeps energy consistent and makes editing far easier (b-roll / screen-recording masks the cut between takes).
- **Honor the marks:** `/` breathe-comma · `//` **stop and let the silence sit** (don't fill it) · `**bold**` punch · `_italic_` slow down · `(breath)` inhale at the idea boundary · `…` trail off.
- **Vocal variety:** lift pitch for energy/urgency, drop it for authority/calm; vary pace and volume; emphasize the marked words. Don't read in a flat line.
- **Mic basics:** consistent distance, slightly **off-axis** to tame plosives (those `p`/`b` pops), and **monitor playback** between takes to catch monotone or rushing.
- **In the edit:** cut filler and dead air, use **j-cuts** (next scene's audio starts under the current visual) to smooth transitions — **but preserve the `//` dramatic pauses.** Don't let the editor strip out the silence that makes a beat land.
- **Pronunciation:** rehearse the §0a list so tricky names (ElevenLabs, DaVinci Resolve, Pexels, Pictory, Fliki) are said the same way every time.

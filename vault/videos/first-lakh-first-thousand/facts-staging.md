# facts-staging — first-lakh-first-thousand

**Currency firewall.** The ₹ set and the $ set below were sourced independently.
The ₹1,00,000 ↔ $10,000 pairing in the creator brief is an **emotional-milestone
equivalence, not a conversion** (₹1 lakh ≈ $1,150 at any real rate). Never state
or imply one is the other, and never carry a rate from one block to the other.

Tags: **HARD** = primary/regulator/published price card, or two independent
top-tier sources agreeing · **SOFT** = single source, survey, or primary
unreachable · **COMPUTED** = arithmetic done here from a tagged input; label
"illustrative" on screen, never speak as a statistic.

**On the URLs below.** A resolvable URL is *not* a verified fetch. Every row
says how it was reached — "read direct" means this stage retrieved the page;
"via search index" or "not fetched" means the URL is recorded for checkability
only, and the row's SOFT tag reflects that. No URL here was constructed or
guessed; each is one this stage actually retrieved or actually saw returned.

**The honest rate to build on:** use **7.1%** (PPF / 3-yr post office TD — the
only two rates in this block that are HARD and that an ordinary saver can
actually get). Equity ~12.4% is **SOFT** — if the script wants it, it must say
"historically, roughly twelve percent" and never a decimal.

### 1.3 COMPUTED — the hero math (illustrative, compute in build code)

₹5,000/mo, monthly compounding. Two rate cases so the script can show that the
rate barely matters at the first milestone.

| | at **0%** | at **7.1%** (PPF/TD, HARD) | at **12.4%** (equity, SOFT) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Months to the **first** ₹1 lakh | **20** | **19** | **18** |
| Months for the **second** lakh (₹1L→₹2L) | 20 | **17** | **15** |
| Months for the **tenth** lakh (₹9L→₹10L) | 20 | **9** | **7** |

- **The hook number: 20 months.** ₹5,000 a month, zero help from returns.
- **The proof of the thesis:** a 12.4% return buys you **two months** on the
  first lakh (20 → 18). On the tenth it buys you **thirteen** (20 → 7). That is
  "100% of the first milestone is your savings rate", stated as arithmetic.
- **Crossover** (the year your money earns more than you contribute; ₹60,000/yr
  in): **≈ ₹8.5 lakh** at 7.1%, **≈ ₹4.8 lakh** at 12.4%.
  ⚠️ **Do not call ₹1 lakh the crossover.** The first lakh is the
  *psychological* milestone; the crossover is roughly **₹5 lakh**. Conflating
  them would be the one factual error this script is most likely to make.

**Munger rule for the script.** The wording is stable across many secondary
sources but **no primary was reachable**: no meeting transcript, no recording,
no verified page number in *Damn Right!*. Therefore:
- Safe: *"Charlie Munger's famous line was that the first hundred thousand is
  the hardest — after that, you can ease off the gas."* (paraphrase, no date,
  no venue, and it dodges the profanity for a monetised cut).
- **Not safe:** "In 1994, at the Berkshire meeting, Munger said…" — the date and
  venue are folk attribution. Do not put a year on screen.
- If the script needs the quote to be load-bearing, it isn't: §2.3's arithmetic
  reaches the same $100,000 independently.

### 2.3 COMPUTED — the hero math (illustrative, compute in build code)

$800/mo (the locked 20% example), monthly compounding.

| | at **0%** | at **4.15%** (top HYSA) | at **10%** (S&P shape) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Months to the **first** $10,000 | **12.5** | **12** | **12** |
| Months for the **second** $10k ($10k→$20k) | 12.5 | 12 | **11** |
| Months for the **tenth** $10k ($90k→$100k) | 12.5 | — | **6** |
| Months to **$100,000** total | 125 (10.4 yrs) | — | **86 (7.2 yrs)** |

- **The stronger US hook is the saving *rate*, not the return.** At the BEA's
  actual national saving rate of **2.7%**, a $4,000 take-home saves **$108/mo** →
  **7.7 years** to the first $10,000 (5.7 years even at 10%). At $800/mo it is
  **12.5 months**. Same person, same market, same returns: the only variable
  that moved is the rate they save at. Both inputs are HARD (BEA + the locked
  example).
- **Crossover** ($9,600/yr in): **≈ $96,000** at 10%, ≈$231,000 at 4.15%.
  **Munger's $100,000 IS the crossover point** — at a $9,600/yr saving rate and
  a ~10% market, the year your money out-earns your contributions arrives at
  about $96,000. This is the video's best beat and it is arithmetic, not a
  quote. Present it as "here is *why* that number" and the SOFT quote becomes
  colour rather than evidence.

## 4. Do not claim (this topic)

- **Any specific APY or fund return on screen**, either market. Standing rule.
- **A date or venue for the Munger quote.**
- **"₹1 lakh is where compounding takes over."** It is not — the crossover is
  ~₹5 lakh (7.1%: ₹8.5 lakh). Say "the first lakh is where the *habit* takes
  over" if the beat is needed.
- **Any months-to-milestone figure spoken as fact.** Every integer in §1.3 and
  §2.3 is model-dependent (compounding frequency, rate assumption). On screen,
  labelled illustrative, with the monthly amount and rate visible in the frame.
- **Cross-market arithmetic.** ₹1 lakh is not $10,000 and no line may imply it.

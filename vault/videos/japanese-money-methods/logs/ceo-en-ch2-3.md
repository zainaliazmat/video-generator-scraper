## Would I keep watching?

Yes — and this is a real rebuild, not a patch. Watched end to end at speed, the
chapter has a shape: a claim (s13), a confirmation (s14), two receipts (s15/s16),
a payoff (s17, `30 TIMES`, the hottest and reddest frame in the chapter, landing
exactly on the argument's turn), a mechanism (s18/s19), an explanation of why the
lie travels (s20), and an out-line that opens a loop (s21). All four Lotties play
and all four carry an idea rather than decorate: 37.8% repeated across a feed, a
row lifted out of a table, a 30:1 measurement, a subset inside a set, one token
radiating and the other still. The strip is not flat — archetypes run
A/A/B/A/B/B/B/A/D/D/A, and the coldest frame (s18, Y=40, near-white paper) sits
against the hottest (s17, red-cast) on either side of the hinge.

**Where attention is at risk: 00:08–00:13.6 (s12).** It is the chapter's only
beat with no number, no drawn art and no new information — kicker `FIRST, THE
NUMBER`, one tease line, over a phone whose screen is a blank dark rectangle
under the .62 grade. It is also the *second* consecutive static `arch-a` mood
frame, so the chapter runs 13.6 seconds and two near-identical layouts before its
first substance arrives (s13's `37.8%` at 14.7s). That is the dip, and it is
where I would leave. Everything after 14s holds.

Luminance across the eleven scenes runs Y=23–40 against chapter 1's Y=18–45 — a
narrower window than the locked chapter, i.e. slightly less dynamic range, but
inside the house grade and not something to spend a render on.

on-screen figure traces: `37.8%` → J2 with the issuing agency's own table on the

institutions in scenes about named institutions. I would be comfortable with
Horioka watching this chapter.

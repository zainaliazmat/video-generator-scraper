THERE IS A FOURTH

And it is the hard one.

RYOAN-JI, KYOTO

A stone basin. Four characters cut into it.

WHAT IT SAYS

What there is, is enough.

The tsukubai inscription at Ryoan-ji, Kyoto

THE DESIGN

All four share one part — the emptiness at the centre.

NONE OF THEM STANDS ALONE

Each needs the emptiness in the middle to be whole.

NOW YOUR LIST

Exam. Job. First salary. Phone. Bike. Flat.

EVERY TIME

The line moved a few weeks after you crossed it.

WHAT HAPPENED

Income rose. So did the definition of "need".

THE WHOLE MARKET

It runs on what you have feeling like less.

THE QUESTION

What is enough for you? Do not answer quickly.

WHY IT MATTERS

The day it is written down, the saving starts by itself.

THE DIFFERENCE

Three tell you how. The fourth tells you what for.

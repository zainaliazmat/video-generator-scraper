## ⛔ The one thing this pack must not get wrong

This video's premise was **corrected during the run** (`run.json.premise_correction`, [[facts-staging]] §1) and the packaging is written to match it:

- It does **not** claim Japanese people are great savers, and it does **not** claim culture explains saving. Horioka (NBER WP 33181, §3 / §9–10) states culture, tradition and national character **are not a major determinant**; Japan's National-Accounts household saving rate was **about 1%** in 2024.
- The hook is **Japan's own two published numbers for the same year**: **37.8%** (Family Income & Expenditure Survey, salaried-worker households) vs **about 1%** (National Accounts, everybody) — one government, one year, a gap Horioka calls "more than 30 times as high".
- The four methods (Mottainai · Hara Hachi Bu · Kakeibo · Taru wo Shiru) are **behavioural tools**, never the cause of a national saving rate.
- **No Japan-vs-India (or Japan-vs-US) saving-rate comparison appears in the title, the description, the tags or the thumbnail.** They are different measures; that error is exactly what this video corrects. The source video's title question — *"जापानी लोग गरीब क्यों नहीं होते?"* — is **retired with the premise and is not restored anywhere below.**

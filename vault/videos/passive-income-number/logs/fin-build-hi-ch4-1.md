### 4 · The cut-in is the one new mechanism in this chapter, and three things about it are declared

**(a) There is no cut-in component in the design system.** Grepped
`tools/scaffold/assets/blockframe.css`, `chapter-design.css`, `motion.js`,
`tools/audio/cues.py` and `tools/pipeline_check.py` for `cutin` / `cut-in` / `bg2`:
`motion.js` mentions cut-in photos only in `fade()`'s own docstring, `cues.py` knows
`#sN-bg\d`, and `pipeline_check` mentions dropped cut-ins in a manifest message. **No
inset frame, no `.cutin` class, nothing.** So it is built as the one mechanism that
exists and that this stage's contract names by hand: a second full-bleed `.bg` at
`opacity:0`, faded in at its keyword's cue and **kenned identically to the layer under
it**, so the change is of FRAMING and never of movement. It therefore REPLACES the
photograph for the rest of the scene rather than insetting over it, and it is never
dissolved back (creator rule, firaun 2026-07-23). **`.bg` is the same class, so the
second file takes the same locked grade** — no per-scene brightness override exists
anywhere in this composition.

**(b) The anchor moved from §10's «दूध और सब्ज़ी» to «सब्ज़ी», because fin-assets
declares the milk is not in the file.** Three fetch rounds returned vegetables with no
milk or Slavic milk still-lifes, and it preferred the honest half to a faked whole. The
picture shows vegetables, so it arrives on the word for vegetables: +3.07 measured, not
+2.55.

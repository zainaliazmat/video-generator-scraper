## Rulings on the declared caveats and deviations

Ruled from the encode, as asked.

- **s19 busy frame** — the busyness is real but it is the least of that scene's problems. Ruled at #1 on subject, which is the finding that matters.
- **s11 pale sky** — **ACCEPT.** The sky is 70% of the frame and Y 55 is the chapter's second-brightest value, but the tank is unmistakable, it is the right country, and it is the only frame in the water run with both an inlet and an outlet visible. The lift out of s10 is the chapter's one intentional tonal move; keep it.
- **s17 numeric keys** — **ACCEPT with a caveat for later chapters.** The dials read 70/50/30/10 and none of those are the figures in the line, but they read as mechanical counter wheels, not as a stated quantity, so they do not assert a wrong number. Watch it if a later chapter puts a figure over the same dials that a viewer could pair with a visible digit.
- **the s10 → s17 adding-machine through-line** — **ACCEPT, it works.** The machine in s10's left background and the counter dials at s17/s18 rhyme without repeating, and they carry "this is arithmetic, not a forecast" better than the ledger page the storyboard specified would have. Keep it.
- **s9 chests vs the ch4 trunk** — **NOT ACCEPTED**, but not for the collision reason. s9 fails on its own line first (#5). Replacing it with the almirah the script asked for dissolves the ch4 collision as a side effect.
- **s15's "The reason comes later."** — **ACCEPT.** It matches the VO («इसकी वजह आगे आएगी») verbatim and, unlike the storyboard's "The reason is in Chapter 5.", it does not promise a chapter number that a later edit could move. The build's version is the better line.
- **the s17 → s18 push-in** — **ACCEPT, and the geometry is verified continuous.** s18.jpg is the 0.9174 crop of s17's source and 1 ÷ 0.9174 = 1.090, which is exactly s17's end scale, so `plateKen(s17, 1.000→1.090)` into `plateKen(s18, 1.000→1.065)` is one unbroken move despite the two calls both starting at 1.000. The storyboard specified the opposite direction; tightening onto the smaller unit as ₹30,000 becomes ₹2,500 is the better reading and it matches s16.

## On the joints

**s10→s11 and s19→s20 are two pictures, and that is correct here.** I am explicitly not carrying
the ch1 s3→s4 ruling forward: there, the storyboard had declared a continuous zoom and the build
overrode it into a dissolve, so the mismatch was a violation. Here both joints are declared `dis`
between two different topics — "the name" to "picture a tank", and "what it buys" to "all twelve
months". Two pictures is what a dissolve between two topics is supposed to look like. Not a finding.

scdet is correctly ignored: it ranks s16's countUp landing (0.592) and s9's statement rise (0.524)
above eleven of twelve joints, so it is scoring type motion, not cuts. It places only the two
declared holds correctly, which is the one thing it is useful for here.

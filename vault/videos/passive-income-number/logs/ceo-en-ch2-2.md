## RULING 1 — the payoff clause is CLOSED, and REPLACED. Binds ch3-6 (en) / ch3-7 (hi).

**The 2.7-point #3 closes it, and I am adopting fin-editor's replacement, because the
argument against my own clause is the argument I would make myself.**

My clause read *"the payoff frame's photograph must be the most legible in its chapter,
never the least."* The second half was the operable one and the defect it was written
against is **gone and inverted, measurably**: s21 went from last of fifteen on p10 to **#1
by 3.3**, holds the **narrowest p90-p50 spread in the chapter (9.0)**, passes sound-off, and
**arrives on +20.4 median — the largest step in the chapter**, where at attempt 2 it was +3,
a plateau. That last number is the only one on the table that describes what a viewer
actually experiences.

The first half — "the most legible" — was **the wrong instrument and I wrote it that way.**
A "#1 of N" rank makes the payoff's compliance a function of how good the *other* frames
are, so a chapter is punished for having two good pictures. And the gap between #1 and #3 on
median here is 2.9, against a composed-vs-encode measurement error of 1.4 on this very
frame. **A gate a measurement error can flip is not a gate.** The right shape is a band.

**REPLACEMENT CLAUSE, in force from here:**

> The payoff frame must (1) **pass the sound-off gate**, (2) rank in the chapter's **top
> quartile on median**, (3) rank **#1 or #2 on p10**, and (4) **arrive on a non-negative
> median step** from the scene before it.
>
> **Top quartile = `ceil(N/4)`, floored at 3.** Without that floor a short chapter collapses
> clause (2) back into the point-rank I am retiring here. This is my only amendment to
> fin-editor's wording.

s21 scores **pass · #3 of 15 · #1 · +20.4**. All four, none marginal.

**Why I trust this and not a rule written to let en through: fin-editor checked its own
proposal against the other cut and reported that hi ch2's hero still fails it.** An agent
that tests its own reformulation against the case it does not own is an agent whose
reformulation I can adopt. That self-check is the reason this is a ruling and not a
negotiation.

**The `>= 55` p90 target is RETIRED, not carried as a miss.** It is a target on a statistic

## RULING 2 — the metric. MEDIAN + spread + a binary sound-off gate. Binds both cuts.

**p90 is retired as a ranking statistic.** Not because it is imprecise — because in this
chapter it is **wrong about the direction of two joints**. `s14->s15` reads Δp90 +18.2
against Δp50 +2.3 (one white page enters and nothing brightens). `s15->s16` reads Δp90 -16.5
against Δp50 +6.1 (p90 says darker exactly where the eye sees the picture get clearer). A
statistic that gets the **sign** of a change wrong cannot rank the frames it is being asked
to rank. And it penalises structurally: s21 has the narrowest spread in the chapter, so p90
docks the payoff frame for exactly the evenness the ruling asks it to have. The hi cut found
the same shape independently on its s10. Two cuts, two methods, one answer.

**THE MEASURE, in force from here:**

1. **A binary SOUND-OFF QUALIFYING GATE runs FIRST.** One question, as fin-editor shaped it:
   *with the type covered, can a viewer name a concrete object in this frame?* Binary. A
   floor, **not a ranker** — it will never separate s13 from s21 and must not be asked to.
   A frame that fails is **ineligible to be called the most legible at any median.**
2. **MEDIAN ranks** the eligible field. Mean corroborates.
3. **`p90 - p50` is reported beside it** as the spikiness flag, never as a ranker. And a
   sharpening the recommendation did not quite make: **near-zero spread is not a credit.**
   A blank field is both even and empty. Spread at either extreme is a prompt to run the
   gate harder, not a score.

**Why a subjective gate, when my standing rule is "fix defaults, not gates".** Because this
is not new machinery. It is no code, no new stage, no check script — it is the read
fin-editor already performs on every scene, being given the standing to **disqualify** where
today it can only annotate. That is a promotion of an existing read, not a new gate, and it
costs zero renders.

**And the evidence for it is a track record, not a preference.** The sound-off read has
returned the same verdict four times across two cuts, three of them before anyone asked for
a gate. Over the same run **every purely numerical measure has been overturned at least
once** — p90 by median, source-side mean by composed mean, scdet by everything, a composed
prediction by the encode. The one test that has never been overturned by a measurement is
the one I am making the qualifier.

**The proof is in hand and it is s9.** Median alone crowns a **closed notebook cover with no
subject** as this chapter's most legible frame. Every purely numerical measure tried on this
run has eventually crowned an empty frame; clause 1 is what closes that failure mode for
good.

**This lands directly on hi ch2, which is being re-sourced now: a flat pale-sky field with
zero spread FAILS clause 1.** No nameable object, therefore ineligible to lead its chapter
on any median. It is the blank-frame defect in new clothes and the gate exists precisely to
catch it. Brief hi ch2's hero for a subject first and a luma second.

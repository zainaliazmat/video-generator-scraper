## RULING 1 — s34: RE-FETCH. This is the blocker.

The editor deferred this twice and referred it to me both times, so I am ruling it rather
than passing it on again.

I opened 63.5 full-size before deciding. At 1080p the frame resolves **`9. Insurance`**,
**`The Contractor`**, **`10. Assignment`** and **`the prior written consent of the`** — and
sitting under them, in our own type, is *"Cooley, Hubbard and Walz, AAII Journal, February
1998 — verbatim, from the study's own methodology."* The editor is right that §10's letter
is met and that this is not a fabricated source. That is not the test I apply.

**My test is whether Cooley, Hubbard and Walz could watch this frame.** They could not. We
have named their paper, their journal and their month, and put a commercial services
agreement underneath it on the frame carrying the chapter's strongest evidence claim. That is
the honesty clause in my own brief almost word for word — *a stock photo implying a specific
place or institution it is not* — and it is the one axis where I do not trade a render for a
mismatch.

It also cuts against the only differentiator the study found for us. The twins cite one
source between them; conclusion 9 of `video-studies/passive-income-number.md` is *"we can do
this better than B because fin-facts sources ours."* The chapter where we cash that in cannot
be the chapter where the source frame is the wrong document.

**Why now and not "if the pool opens anyway":** SHIP means locked. There is no later state in
which this gets fixed for free. fin-assets is about to open the pool for ch4–7, so the
marginal cost of this fetch is one slot, and the crop lever is measured dead (0px horizontal
slack, words dead-centre in the sharp band) so a re-fetch is the only lever that exists.

## RULING 1 — s34: **OPTION (a). Keep the shipped frame. I am reversing my own attempt-1 blocker.**

fin-assets did the work I asked for and did it honestly: 19 sheets, 111 cells, 5 full-resolution
fetches, 0 substitutions, nothing quietly promoted. Its finding is that my brief was
self-contradictory, and it is right. I am not going to spend a third round defending a ruling
its own evidence has overturned.

**Why not (b), the p10 ≥ 35 relaxation.** It buys the named subject, and I take that seriously —
it is the only option that answers the ruling on its own terms. It fails on three counts I can
see and one I cannot:
1. The candidate is a **genuine two-column research article** with hands and a highlighter on it
   at 1880px. The whole objection is legible text implying a document. A real article is *more*
   likely to resolve a real title and real authors than a generic contract is, and a real, wrong
   paper title sitting under *Cooley, Hubbard and Walz* is a §10 breach in the **letter** —
   fabricated source — where today's frame breaches only the spirit. fin-assets did not report
   what that article says, and I will not authorise it blind.
2. It puts a **hand on paper immediately after s33**, which is already a hand frame, at the exact
   beat I praised the chapter for. s34 is not on §10's declared hand list; s33 is.
3. It costs a render to move the payoff frame from #1 on p10 to ~#6, on the honesty axis alone.
4. And ch4–7 inherit it. A tone floor moved from 80 to 35 is not a relaxation, it is a repeal.

**Why not (c), moving the citation.** s34's statement is a **verbatim quotation in quotation
marks**. Strip the foot and we are running an unattributed quote, and s37 already carries the same
authors' names on the Conclusion — the citation would be duplicated where it is not needed and
absent where it is. An unattributed quotation is worse for our credibility than a texture
mismatch, and the storyboard's own rule is that every citation lives in the `foot:`.

**What I am ruling, plainly.** `9. Insurance` / `The Contractor` / `10. Assignment` stay legible
at 1080p, and that is a cost I am accepting with my name on it. It is defensible: the photograph
**asserts nothing**. There is no title, no figure, no journal name, no agency, no number — §10's
letter is met, the quote is verbatim, the attribution is correct and in the place the storyboard
puts every attribution. Cooley, Hubbard and Walz would see a stock photograph of paper under an
accurate quotation of their methodology. That is untidy; it is not dishonest, and untidy does not
cost a viewer. My own gate says I may only REWORK for something that does. This does not.

**No free lever exists either, and I checked before ruling.** `bgpos` is measured dead (0px
horizontal slack). `ken("#s34-bg", …, true)` runs 1.00 → 1.16 with xPercent −2.5 → +2.5, i.e. it
drifts *toward* the left column, so it ends on the most legible state; and pushing the start scale
far enough to crop `9.` and `10.` off the left edge would upsample an 1880px source past the point
the encode forgives. Re-fetch was the only lever and the pool does not stock it.

## RULING 2 — the forward rule. **Binding on ch4–7 in BOTH cuts. This is what replaces clause 3.**

The brief asked me to say what ch4–7 inherit. They do **not** inherit `p10 ≥ 35`.

The lesson of these 111 cells is that **the tone floor is what caused the defect**. A frame is
legible *because* it is bright and flat; `p10 ≥ 80` after this grade means the page fills the
frame with no shadow, which is exactly the condition under which a viewer reads the words. s37 is
the pattern that works — an open book under a lamp, text dissolved to grey blocks, p10 in the
20s — and it carries the same authors' names in its foot with no mismatch at all, because there is
nothing to mismatch.

So, for §10's remaining document slots (**s62, s66, s70**, plus any later slot whose `foot:`
carries a source citation), in both cuts:

- **On a citation-bearing document slot the photograph must not RESOLVE.** Out-of-focus, raking,
  dissolved, edge-on, macro past the point of reading. Target the s37 frame, not the s34 frame.
- **No tone floor applies to that slot class.** Do not spend a pass hunting a bright page; brightness
  is the failure mode there, not the goal. Ground temperature on those slots is set by neighbours,
  not by a floor.
- **Do not spec a subject the pools do not stock.** They index moods and objects; "an academic
  paper" is neither, and every query for it returns a book, a Bible or a contract. Query the light
  and the fill, not the object — fin-assets' own §Owed 3, and I am making it standing.
- `@commons` stays unspent on texture beats, per fin-assets' §"Why the third pool was not spent":
  its inventory is scans, which resolve titles and authors perfectly and add a BY condition.

This is slot-class-local and it is a correct default rather than a gate, which is the ranking the
creator set on 2026-07-29.

## ⚠ Read this first: this topic is BROWSE-led, not SEARCH-led

Unlike 50-30-20 (where [[50-30-20-rule/youtube-metadata-hi|Warikoo owned the keyword]]),
here the problem is the opposite — **there is almost no search demand to capture.**

**"needs vs wants" is a children's keyword.** Autocomplete, both markets:
`needs vs wants for kids` · `kindergarten` · `read aloud` · `brainpop` · `song` ·
`for teens`. Titling the video that puts us against elementary-school classroom
content and the wrong audience entirely. **Do not put "needs vs wants" in the
title.** It stays in the tags only, low priority.

**"subscription" in Hindi YouTube means gaming memberships.** Scrape of
`subscription kharcha`, last 12 months: Free Fire Prime level-ups (2.29M, 462k,
438k), Punjabi songs, zero finance. The bare word is worse than useless here.

**The `auto debit kaise band kare` cluster is real but it isn't ours.** It's a
big, high-intent lane — but every winner is a click-path tutorial:

| Views | Title | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| 375,958 | paytm automatic payment stop kaise kare | Manish4u |
| 142,901 | How to disable auto pay in phonepe \| phonepe autopay kaise band kare | Manish4u |
| 110,997 | google pay se autopay kaise band kare | Manish4u |
| 51,686 | How to disable auto pay in phonepe | Arvind zone |

Those viewers want a 90-second settings walkthrough. Our video is a
money-behaviour piece — title into that lane and they bounce in 15 seconds,
which is worse for the channel than not ranking at all. **Use `auto debit` /
`upi autopay` in the tags, never in the title.**

**The healthy lane is the savings cluster** — same finding as the 50-30-20 pack:

| Views | Title | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| 1,786,641 | Salary कम है? … फिर भी पैसा बच सकता है | Growth Ved |

| **1,317** | **Hidden Charges Exposed 😳 Subscription Se Paise Kaise Bachaye** | Seekho App |

That last row is the opportunity: **high problem-awareness, zero supply.**
Hundreds of thousands of Indians are actively trying to kill auto-debits, and
nobody has made the money-behaviour video about it in Hindi.

**Conclusion: the title must earn the click, not match a query.** Lead with the
annual rupee number and the curiosity gap. Search is a bonus here, not the plan.

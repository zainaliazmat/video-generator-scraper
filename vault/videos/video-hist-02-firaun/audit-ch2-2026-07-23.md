## Findings, ranked

**1. Firaun's face — RESOLVED (creator chose A, 2026-07-23): visible, locked villain.**
Correction to the first read: P08a is **not** a violation. [[DESIGN]] rule 3 already reversed the old
"no clear Firaun face" rule (2026-07-19) with a verbatim **FIRAUN FACE LOCK**, and the lock is present
in all 7 Firaun prompts (P08a, P08b, P19c, P31a, P31c, P38a, P41b). The "violation" was a **stale
leftover** in the DESIGN glow-rule paragraph + the storyboard P08a spec still saying "from behind" —
both now fixed to point to the lock. **Consistency QA (the real risk of choice A):** the 7 generated
Firaun stills read as the same lean, hook-nosed ~50-yr-old; distant throne shots (P08b/P31a/P31c) are
too small to matter; the close ones (P08a, P19c, P38a, P41b) match in facial structure — the
bald-vs-nemes difference is contextual (battle/tower vs court), correct. **Acceptable as-is.** If ever
tightening is wanted, drive future Firaun stills from one reference image (img2img/seed), not text
alone. No image change needed; docs cleaned up.

**2. The chapter opens soft, right after the hook's cliffhanger (retention cliff).**
The hook ends on max curiosity (the goat, the reopened mystery); ch2 opens on a calm misty brickfield +
"it's morning." Gorgeous but low-tension exposition — the energy flatlines for ~12s. Carry a thread
from the hook, or open seg 06 on the hardest image (whip-shadow / stone-haul) + a tension line, THEN
settle into the brickfield calm. Don't spend the first 12s at rest.

**3. The divinity claim (08.4, 79:24) is the chapter's shock peak and under-punched.**
A man claiming to *be God* — «میں تمہارا سب سے بڑا رب ہوں» — is the most arresting line in the chapter
and the ideological hook of the whole film (tyrant vs God). It currently rides a held crowd shot. Give
it a **locked beat + a breath of silence + a keyword punch card** so it lands like a gut-punch, per the
storyboard's own "claim lands in near-silence" note.

**4. The throne sequence dwells ~22s (0:35–0:57) on one location.** Throne + crowd + hand across 5
lines = the middle-sag risk. The continuous zoom helps, but vary scale/angle harder: a low push past
bowed heads to the dais, a hard cut to the ringed hand, a top-down of the prostrate hall.

**5. Exposition density in 07.2** (children of Yusuf / famine backstory) is the most "history-lesson"
line — the drift point. Keep the picture moving; consider tightening the VO.

## Per-chapter

### Ch4 «مدین سے طُور تک» (5:00) — the biggest retention risk
Theologically the richest chapter (the killing + flight, Midian well, 10-yr shepherding, the fire, **Tuwa
/ Kalim-Allah**, staff→serpent, the glowing hand, the mission, "I am with you", speak gently). But:
- **5 minutes is too long for a middle chapter** and it carries **10 dwells ≥13s** — the worst in the film.
- The reflective/exposition stretches drift: **16b** (shepherding-as-training philosophy) and **17.7–17.11**
  (the long "speak gently / the door stays open" coda) are the drift points.
- **Fixes, ranked:** (1) tighten the VO — compress 16b and the 17 coda; (2) break the 10 dwells with
  alternate stills (Tuwa 16c is intentionally reverent-locked — leave it); (3) the strong beats (punch,
  night flight, the fire, staff→serpent, glowing hand) should hit harder and sooner. This chapter most
  needs a length pass before a dwell pass.

### Ch5 «چرواہا اور بادشاہ» (2:05) — strong
Court return, **Haman's tower** hubris ("build me a tower to peek at Musa's God"), the magicians' duel,
the staff swallows the magic, **the magicians' turn** (sujood + "you rule only this world") — a great
emotional pivot. Only 2 dwells (P22a 14s sujood, P23a 17s). Keep; break the two holds.

### Ch6 «نشانیوں پر نشانیاں» (3:01) — the list chapter
The 9 signs one by one, the broken-promise loop (5×), then "why deny? — arrogance." Risk = list monotony,
mitigated by the **9-signs tally card** (good progress device) and the frog-beat humour. 4 moderate
dwells (P25b 17s etc.). **Fix:** keep each sign tight; the tally card is doing real work — make sure it
visibly ticks each beat. Blood-Nile is the climax sign — give it the most weight.

### Ch7 «رات کا سفر + سمندر» (1:51) — the best-paced chapter
The exodus, the trap, Musa's faith line («میرا رب میرے ساتھ ہے»), the **sea split (P36, the money shot)**,
the collapse (37) with 3s silence. **Zero long dwells.** This is the model for the rest — lots of distinct
shots, escalating. Leave it alone.

### Ch8 «آخری سانس + تیرا بدن بچا لیں گے» (1:52) — has the video's thesis; two problems
The drowning confession, **«اب؟» (NOW? — the door closes)**, "today We save your body as a sign", Ibn
Kathir (body thrown ashore), "for those AFTER — us too", bridge to the goat.
- **① P39 is MISSING** (39.1–39.8, 8 lines held on the drowning still) — and it's the **thematic peak of the
  whole film** ("NOW? — repentance's door isn't always open"). It MUST get its own image. **Highest-priority
  single fix in ch4–10.**
- **② P38c-alt1 held 30.5s** — the longest single dwell in the film (the drowning aftermath).
- **Fix:** generate P39 (a shut door / the closing hand / darkening water — a dread image for "اب؟"), and
  break the 30s aftermath dwell.

### Ch9 «3000 سال بعد — بکری + پیرس» (2:26) — ties back to the hook
The goat + Abd el-Rassul cache (1881), 50+ mummies, Ramesses II, the 1976 Paris trip (matches the hook's
France footage), the 3 myth-busts (passport / salt / no-embalming) as stamp-card pattern-interrupts. Good.
3 dwells (P46a 21s). **Fix:** break P46a; the myth-busts are a strong device — keep.

### Ch10 «نشانی آج بھی + آخری بات» (2:18) — strong close, long holds
**Intellectual honesty is the standout:** "is it the same Firaun? Honestly — no one knows for sure; the
Quran named no one; whoever claims certainty claims more than the Quran." That candour builds huge trust
and pre-empts "debunk" comments — a real asset. Then the moral (arrogance's end preserved), the door
recap, the CTA («نشانی» comment), and the Thamud teaser.
- **Fix:** P53a (the close/CTA) is held **28.6s** and P50a **20s** — break the closing dwells so the
  send-off doesn't sag; the ending should build, not idle on one frame.

# Audit — Chapter 3 «دریا میں صندوق» (the chest in the river)

**Verdict:** narratively the **strongest chapter yet** — a real emotional arc with a great button
(«کمال ہے نا؟»). Two prior worries are already fixed: the Quran word is *tabut* = **wooden chest**,
so the chapter is retitled صندوق and the VO uses صندوق (not ٹوکری/basket) throughout; and **P13b now
exists** (the dramatic-irony pickup that was flagged missing). Depiction is clean — the glow-rule is
correctly applied (Musa's mother + Aasiya get the white-glow face; ordinary wet-nurses keep faces).
The one real weakness is **pacing: the chapter parks on single images for 17–21s at a stretch.**

## Findings, ranked

**1. Three very long single-image dwells — the chapter's main retention risk.**
- **P11a** ~19s (0:05–0:24, the mother+chest interior, glow-face) — the chapter opens by staring at
  one shot for ~19s.
- **P13b** ~17s (0:49–1:06, the chest resting on the palace steps).
- **P15b** ~21s (0:35 → the reunion climax, mother+baby held for ~21s straight).
The continuous-zoom fix keeps them smooth, but 17–21s on one composition three times reads as static —
the eye finishes the image in ~4s and then waits. **Fix:** give each long beat 1–2 alternate stills to
cut between so the zoom has fresh frames — e.g. a macro of the baby's hand / the chest's pitch-and-rope
detail / the water lapping / the mother's hands (11); the chest small at the foot of the looming palace
+ a reed/step detail (13); **the calming baby's face-glow, the mother's arms, a nurse's reaction, Aasiya
watching (15)**. The reunion is the emotional peak — it deserves 3 shots, not 21s on one.

**2. The dramatic irony (13.3) is the chapter's best idea and under-sold visually.** "The chest comes
to rest at the very palace steps the kill-order came from." Make the palace **loom** menacingly over the
tiny chest (scale = dread), and consider a 0.5s intercut of the ch2 throne/soldier-shadow to bind
"the throne that decreed death → life floats to its doorstep." Right now it's a calm chest-on-steps.

**3. The reason-vs-faith beat (12.5–12.7) is a strong psychological moment — protect it.** "Reason says:
this is death. Faith says: this is protection. She chose faith." It rides the chest-tiny-on-vast-water
frame (good). Punctuate «اُس ماں نے ایمان کو چنا» with a held beat of stillness/silence before seg 13.

**4. Glow-face is visually heavy this chapter** (mother in 11, Aasiya in 14, mother in 15 — three
intimate held shots on a bright white face-blob). Rule-compliant and unavoidable given three
never-a-face women, but it's a lot of glow; lean on the script's HANDS-ONLY option (already used at
11.1, 14.1) for some of the long holds to vary it, rather than holding the glow-face for 20s.

## What works (keep)
- **Story + structure** — reason-vs-faith, the irony, Aasiya's mercy, the reunion reveal («اُس کی اپنی
  ماں») and the «کمال ہے نا؟» button. Best-written chapter; the payoff genuinely lands.
- **Wooden chest** correct and consistently rendered (the tabut fix held).
- **Glow-rule** applied correctly (revered women glow; ordinary nurses have faces).
- Consistent grade; smooth motion (flicker fix carried over); P13b gap closed.

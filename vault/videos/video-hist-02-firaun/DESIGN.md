# DESIGN — فرعون کا انجام (film rules)

**Concept:** an investigation told as cinema. Act 1 is a lived story (the Quran narrative,
dramatic and warm); Act 2 is a forensic mystery (the mummy, cold and modern). The signature
emotion is the HANDOFF between them — the same "sign" travelling 3000 years. Engagement
engine = the script's 7 loops + the era-jump timeline ribbon + the myth-postmortem payoff
+ the ibrah close.

**Format:** 1920×1080, `data-fps=30`, **~22 min** (target 21:30–22:30). H.264 MP4.
Every scene's duration locks to the VO line it carries (TTS clips, one per line).

## ⚠️ DEPICTION RULES — BINDING, override every other rule
**THE #1 RULE (creator, 2026-07-18, emphatic): we NEVER show the face of a prophet or a
revered Islamic figure. Never, ever.** This is non-negotiable and outranks every visual/
composition goal. If a shot can't be built without risking a face, change the shot.

1. **NEVER-A-FACE list — these figures have NO face, NO identifiable likeness, in ANY frame:**
   - **Musa (AS)** and **Haroon (AS)** — prophets.
   - **Musa's family** — his **mother**, his **sister**, (and any household member).
   - **Aasiya** (Firaun's wife) — the revered believing woman who raised Musa.
   - **The old man of Madyan** (may be Shu'ayb AS — treat as a prophet to be safe) and, out
     of the same modesty, **his two daughters**.
   **➤ THE GLOW RULE (creator, 2026-07-19) — how these figures MAY now appear on screen.**
   A figure on the NEVER-A-FACE list may be shown in frame, including front-on, **provided the
   face is replaced by a soft radiant WHITE GLOW** — a smooth luminous veil of white light exactly
   where the face would be, carrying **no eyes, no mouth, no nose, no features and no facial
   outline** readable through it, blending softly at the edges into hair, headcloth and shoulders.
   The glow is the depiction-safe substitute for a face; body, robes, hands and posture are shown
   normally. This unlocks front-facing, emotionally readable staging (a mother holding her child,
   two brothers walking into a throne room) that the strict back-turned rule could not give.
   **Applies to: Musa (AS), Haroon (AS), Musa's mother, Musa's sister, Aasiya.**
   **Does NOT apply to Firaun or any other character** (creator explicit): Firaun gets **NO glow**.
   His **face IS shown** as an ordinary character — see rule 3 + the FIRAUN FACE LOCK below. (This
   supersedes the old "from behind / face never clearly shown" treatment — creator A-decision
   2026-07-23; the lock, not facelessness, is how consistency is kept.) Ordinary Egyptians, soldiers,
   magicians, crowds, servants and the 1881/1976/modern characters are shown normally, no glow, no
   special restriction.
   ⚠️ OPEN — creator to confirm: the **old man of Madyan** (possibly Shu'ayb AS) **and his two
   daughters**. Until confirmed they stay on the CONSERVATIVE treatment already built — the elder
   as hands-only/no head in frame, the daughters distant and back-turned — since the creator's list
   did not name them. Say the word and they get the glow instead.
   Back-turned, hands-only, POV and empty-frame compositions remain fully valid and are still the
   right choice where they are simply the better shot (P17b's shadows, P16d1's empty tree).
   Other ways they may appear: objects they hold (staff, sandals, a chest, a water jar);
   **hands only**; **backs turned / from behind**; **extreme-wide** where no single figure
   reads as an identity; **POV** (we see what they see); or an **empty, beautifully-lit frame**
   the VO speaks over. When in doubt → shoot the object, the hands, or the landscape. NO
   silhouette that functions as "this is him/her" either — a recognizable single-person
   silhouette is still a depiction. Crowd-scale anonymous figures (e.g. the Exodus column,
   the magicians falling in sujood as a distant mass) are OK only when NO individual is
   framed as a named person.
2. **No depiction of the divine. Ever.** The Tuwa scene = fire-that-is-light in a tree,
   no smoke, no form, no voice-source visual. Sandals on rock. Light on the valley floor.
3. **Firaun's face IS now shown** (creator, 2026-07-19 — REVERSES the 07-18 "never clearly shown"
   rule). He is not a revered figure and is treated as an ordinary character with a fully visible
   face. He gets **NO glow**.
   **⚠️ FIRAUN FACE LOCK — paste this description VERBATIM into every prompt he appears in, or the
   generator will invent a different pharaoh each time:**
   > *an Egyptian king of about fifty, the SAME man in every image: a lean hard clean-shaven face,
   > high flat cheekbones, a strong straight nose with a slight downward hook, heavy-lidded dark
   > eyes, a thin unsmiling mouth, a short square chin, deep vertical furrows between the brows,
   > bronzed weathered skin, wearing the striped blue-and-gold nemes headcloth with a rearing
   > golden cobra at the brow*
   Appears in: P08a, P08b, P19c, P31a, P31c, P38a, P41b. **P08c and P31b stay hand-only detail
   shots** — a hand on the throne arm is still the better image and needs no face.
   **Exception — the shoreline (P41):** his face may be visible but stays **intact, calm and
   undamaged**, eyes closed, at dawn. No wounds, no bloating, no decay, no horror grammar. The beat
   is "the body preserved as a sign", not a corpse shot.
   **QA:** compare each generated Firaun image against the lock above; regenerate on mismatch.
4. **The real mummy is shown respectfully, museum-context only** — display case, scanning
   room, archival transport. Face withheld through the hook; even at the reveal, museum
   framing only. No lingering decay close-ups, no horror grammar.
5. **No Arabic text on screen** (creator v2 rule) — Quran beats render as Urdu translation
   on parchment + cite chip. No nudity, no gore (the punch and the drownings are implied).

**Every image prompt MUST carry a negative clause** enforcing this, e.g.: *"no human face
visible for the central figure; show hands / from behind / from a distance only; no
depiction of any prophet's or revered figure's face."* Bake it into each prompt — non-agent
mode means each prompt stands alone (see image-prompts rules).

## Typography (embed in HyperFrames — never system-ui)
- **On-screen Urdu (ALL of it — canonical rule 2026-07-18): Noto Nastaliq Urdu.**
  Cards/translations 44–60 px, `--ink` on parchment `#2b2418` ink; line-height ≥2.0
  (Nastaliq needs vertical room — test descenders before locking card heights).
  Kinetic type = word-by-word opacity/y rises, NEVER letter-spacing animation (breaks Nastaliq joining).
- **Cite chips:** small Nastaliq chip bottom-right — «القصص 28:4» style; `--ink` on faint dark band. Every Quran/source beat gets one. This is the channel's "verified" brand mark.
- **Numerals/dates (1881, 1976):** Archivo Black, huge, per channel system.
- **English (titles-card at end, RAMESSES II name card):** Playfair Display.

## Motion language
- Ken Burns every recon still `1.0 → 1.08–1.14`, sine.inOut, alternating direction.
- 2.5D parallax: foreground drifts more; text/UI ~25–35% of image travel.
- **Camera = emotion:** push-in = intimacy/dread (09, 38–39) · pull-back = scale (30 blood-Nile,
  50 museum hall) · tilt-up = the tower (19), the water walls (36) · **locked** on silence
  beats (12 basket, 20-21 swallow, 39 «اب؟», 52 final case).
- Transitions: 1.0 s cross-dissolve default · hard-cut-to-black + 1 beat silence ONLY at the
  4 pattern interrupts (20-21, 37, 39, 47-48 stamps) · era jumps use the ribbon + a 0.5 s
  exposure bloom.
- **The drowning (37–39):** the one sequence allowed heavy simulated motion — churning dark,
  bubbles, light fading up-frame; then total stillness at dawn. Stillness is the payoff.
- **⚠️ Same still across consecutive lines = ONE continuous shot (build rule, learned 2026-07-23).**
  One-scene-per-line means a held image would otherwise be re-rendered as a fresh scene and
  cross-dissolved *with a copy of itself* at a mismatched zoom → a luminance **dip + scale pop** the
  creator reads as **flicker**. `build.py` now groups consecutive same-image scenes, **cuts invisibly**
  between them (scale matched at the cut), and runs **one continuous linear zoom** (`zc`, ease none)
  across the whole run (groups alternate in/out). Never cross-dissolve an image with itself.
- **Dissolves are dip-free:** the incoming opaque photo fades in *over* the outgoing held at full
  opacity (no black through the mid-point). Reserve dissolves for **real** image changes only.
- **A text card is an OVERLAY, never a full-frame background (learned 2026-07-23).** Kinetic Urdu rides
  a moving still via the `.ov` path + `CARD_BG` map; the card panel stays fixed (only the photo scales)
  so text stays legible. No card holds >~4s without the frame changing; on-screen text is a VO-synced
  keyword punch, not the full spoken sentence. The image-dropping full-card (`C:`) path flattened 59%
  of the ch1 hook — don't use it for narrative beats. See [[hook-critique-2026-07-23]].

## Sound design

  reverent near-silence under the kalam beats.
- The collapse (37): full sound, then 3 s of nothing (VO silent too — scripted).
- 1881 = lantern creaks, rope, wind; 1976 = projector rattle under the newsreel look.
- **VO = TTS per [[../../workflows/voiceover-tts]]:** ONE clip per script line, ≥0.5 s
  pauses (timeline by construction), Nastaliq master → Devanagari → ElevenLabs, per-zone
  settings, nukta check. Duck music −8 dB under VO. Reverent pacing on 16c and 51–52.
- Music: cleared / YT Audio Library only. Two cues: ancient-story cue (warm, modal) +
  investigation cue (pulse, restrained); both resolve into one closing theme at 51.

## Realism & continuity rules for the AI stills
1. **Fact-check every scene before prompting** (standing rule): NMEC hall layout, Le Bourget
   1976 look, TT320 shaft geometry, Madyan well-country terrain, Egyptian mudbrick texture —
   verify from real references first, one-shot over-specified prompts, photoreal never CGI.
2. **Character consistency debt = near zero by design** (faces are avoided per depiction
   rules). The only recurring "character" needing a lock is the old brickmaker's HANDS/robe
   (06) if reused, and THE horse-free equivalents here: the staff + sandals props — keep
   identical across scenes (same wood grain, same strap style).
3. **Light tracks the story clock:** dawn mist (06) → noon glare (20-21) → night torches
   (30–33) → sunrise trap (35) → underwater gloom (38) → still dawn (39–41).
4. Film grain over everything, vignette, faint paper texture — persistent top tracks.
5. No anachronisms; no legible modern text inside recon frames; no pyramids-everywhere
   cliché (Ramesside Egypt = temples, mudbrick, the river — pyramids were already ancient).

## Motion — STILL IMAGES + HyperFrames motion (creator decision 2026-07-19 — REVERSES the 07-18 text-to-video call)
**Text-to-video is abandoned: too expensive, and every wrong generation costs another full clip's
credits.** Every scene is a **still image**, and scenes get **MULTIPLE stills where motion is
wanted** — different angles/scales/details of the same moment (e.g. a wide, a low water-level mid,
a macro detail), cut between and animated in HyperFrames with Ken Burns, 2.5D parallax and
dissolves. Cheaper per attempt, and a bad still costs one retry, not a clip.
Prompts: **`image-prompts.txt`** (plain text, "Scene Pxx —", separated by ---), built CHAPTER BY
CHAPTER with per-chapter web research logged in storyboard.md. Each prompt = one fully
self-contained still: subject, wardrobe, materials, architecture, props, light, colour, era, lens
and composition — **no camera-move sentence** — plus the no-face negative clause.
`video-prompts.txt` is SUPERSEDED (kept only as reusable scene-description source).
**⚠️ The no-face rule still governs every prompt.** QA every image — discard + regen if a face
appears. Cards (P24, P39, P40, P47–49, P51, P53) stay HyperFrames-built. Highest-motion scenes
(P34, P36, P37) get the most stills — cover them with 3–4 angles each so the cut carries the energy.
## Determinism / render contract (non-negotiable)
One `gsap.timeline({paused:true})` on `window.__timelines["<id>"]`; no
`Date.now()`/`performance.now()`/unseeded `Math.random()`/network at render; no `repeat:-1`;
animate only `x,y,scale,rotation,opacity,color,backgroundColor,borderRadius`; root explicitly
sized; full-bleed fills; `<video>`/`<audio>` direct children of root; `hyperframes lint`/
`validate`/`inspect` at 0 errors before preview. Chapter-wise build per
[[../../skills/hyperframes_production]] §5c: build → proof → re-render each chapter
standalone; concat only when all locked. Pipeline stages: [[../video-hist-01-travel/production-runbook]] §6.

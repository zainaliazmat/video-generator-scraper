## SCRIPT (VO + visual cues)

### HOOK — 0:00–1:20 · [ordinary-life cold open, dramatic irony]

**01** — Ak admi bread khareed raha ha. Garam, taaza bread — bilkul waisa hi jasa
ap aaj khareedta ho.
`[AI reconstruction: Roman bakery counter, haath bread pakarta hova, warm morning light]`

**02** — Ak aurat fast food ki dukan pa kharee ha, or dukandar sa behas kar rahi
ha ka kal ka stew ma tha kya.
`[AI recon: thermopolium counter, dolia pots, argument mid-gesture]`

**03** — Shehar ka kinara pa ak villa ka astabal ma, ak naukar ak shaandaar,
taaqatwar ghora saaf kar raha ha. Ya koi aam ghora nahi — ya kisi ameer aadmi
ki shaan ha.
`[AI recon: a groom brushing a large, well-bred horse in a villa stable at
first light — THE horse shot, hold 3s. Sets up the Civita Giuliana payoff.]`

**04** — Ak gladiator apni subah ki training sa pehla bazu pa tel mal raha ha.
Or ak school ma bacha ustaad ka samna wax ki takhti sa parh raha hain.
`[AI recon: 2 quick cuts — gladiator yard · open-front school on street]`

**05** — Ya sab log ak aam si subah guzaar raha hain. Or in ma sa kisi ak ko bhi
nahi pata... ka aaj sham tak in ka pura shehar is dunya sa mit jaya ga.
`[slow push-in on the street; distant Vesuvius enters frame top-right — first
time we see the mountain. Music: low drone starts]`

**06** — Ya 79 AD ha. Shehar ka naam — Pompeii. Or jis pahar ka neecha ya log
2,000 saal sa reh raha hain na, aaj wo pahar phatna wala ha.
`[wide: full city + Vesuvius looming. Title card burns in: POMPEII KA AKHRI DIN]`

**07** — Lakin suno — ya video eruption ka bara ma nahi ha. Ya us SUBAH ka bara
ma ha jo eruption sa pehla thi. Kyun? Kyun ka asal kahani wohi ha. Or is video
ka akhir ma ma ap ko wo cheezain dikhaon ga jo scientists ko abhi — yani 2024
or 2025 ma — mili hain. Aisi cheezain jinho na 2,000 saal purani is kahani ko
dobara likh diya ha. Theek ha? To chalo — us din ki subah 6 baja chalta hain.
`[reframe card, parchment texture: "ERUPTION NAHI. US SA PEHLI SUBAH." — then
timestamp chip appears bottom-left: "6:00 AM" (stays as recurring UI)]`

### SETUP — 1:20–3:10 · Rome ka sona shehar

**08** — Pehla ya samjho ka Pompeii tha kya. Us waqt Roman Empire aadhi dunya pa
raaj karti thi. Or Pompeii? Naples ki khaari ka kinara pa basa hova empire ka
sab sa khoobsurat kona.
`[3D map: Roman Empire extent → zoom to Bay of Naples → Pompeii pin. How So-style
textured map, date chip "79 AD"]`

**09** — Yani agar aaj ka zamana ma socho, to Pompeii ak beach city thi — ameer
logon ka villas, port pa aata hova jahaz, 100 sa zyada dukanain or bars. Koi
15 sa 20 hazar log.
`[AI recon: harbor + villas montage, lively]`

**10** — Or shehar ka upar, sirf 8-9 kilometer door, ak bara sabz pahar —
Vesuvius. Log is sa darta nahi thay. Balka pyar karta thay. Kyun? Kyun ka is ki
dhalwanon ki mitti itni zarkhez thi ka angoor ka baghat wahan sab sa acha
hota thay.
`[slow tilt up the green mountain, vineyards on slopes — the "friendly mountain"]`

**11** — Ab ya suno. Romans ki zaban ma... volcano ka koi lafz hi nahi tha.
Socho zara. Jo cheez unho na kabhi dakhi hi nahi thi, us ka naam kya rakhta?
Un ka liya Vesuvius bas ak pahar tha. Hamesha sa khamosh. Hamesha sa mehrban.
`[parchment card: "VOLCANO = ?" — Latin dictionary style, empty entry. Beat of
silence after "mehrban"]`

**12** — Han, 17 saal pehla — 62 AD ma — ak zabardast zalzala zaroor aya tha.
Aadha shehar gir gaya tha. Log ab tak marammat kar raha thay. Lakin kisi na us
zalzala ko pahar sa nahi jora. Kasa jorta? Volcano hota kya ha, ya pata hi
nahi tha.
`[ruins footage: repaired walls, different brick layers visible — the evidence
layer. Label: "62 AD ka zalzala — marammat ab tak jari"]`

### THE MORNING — 3:10–7:30 · subah 6 sa 12 · [hour-by-hour begins]

**13** — Chalo ab wapis us subah pa. 6 baja. Suraj nikla ha, or shehar jaag
raha ha.
`[timestamp chip: "6:00 AM". Golden-hour street, shutters opening]`

**14** — Sab sa pehla jaagta hain nanbai — yani bakers. Ak bakery ma tandoor
garam ho chuka ha or 81 roti andar lagi hain. Ya number ma na khud nahi banaya
— 81 rotiyan aaj bhi wahin hain. Jali hui, patthar bani hui, lakin poori ki
poori mehfooz. 2,000 saal baad bhi.
`[AI recon: baker loading oven → MATCH CUT to museum photo of the real
carbonized loaves. This is the signature edit of the video]`

**15** — 7 baja. Dukanain khulti hain. Pompeii ma koi 80 thermopolia thay —
yani fast food counters. Garam khana, sasti wine. Zyada tar logon ka gharon ma
kitchen tha hi nahi, to sara shehar bahar khata tha.
`[timestamp "7:00 AM". AI recon: counter with round dolia holes → ruins footage
of the real painted thermopolium counter]`

**16** — Or deewarain? Deewarain bolti thin. Pompeii ki deewaron pa 11,000 sa
zyada likhi hui cheezain mili hain. Election ka nara. Mohabbat ka paigham.
Gaaliyan. Ak jaga to kisi na likha — "ma yahan tha". Bilkul waisa jasa aaj log
likhta hain. Theek ha? Insan kabhi nahi badla.
`[parchment card: real graffiti examples with Urdu translation lines appearing
one by one — kinetic type]`

**17** — 8 baja. School ka time. Ustaad kursi pa, bacha stool pa, or school
gali ki taraf sa bilkul khula. Parhai or guzarta hova log — beech ma kuch
bhi nahi.
`[timestamp "8:00 AM". AI recon: open-front school]`

**18** — 9-10 baja hamam ka waqt ha. Roman hamam yani bath house — wahan ka
gym bhi tha, swimming pool bhi, or gossip ka adda bhi. Or usi waqt shehar ka
bahar amphitheater ma gladiators practice kar raha hain. 20,000 logon ka
shehar, or stadium bhi 20,000 ka. Poora shehar samata tha.
`[timestamp "9:00 AM". AI recon: baths steam, then aerial: the real
amphitheater ruins → recon overlay fills it with crowd — layer transition]`

**19** — Ab ma ap ko wo cheez bataon jo is poori kahani ka sab sa dardnaak
hissa ha. Pichla kuch dinon sa chota chota zalzala aa raha thay. Kuwon ka pani
sookh gaya tha. Janwar bechain thay. Yani zameen... khabardar kar rahi thi.
`[tone shift: music drops. Slow shots — dry well, uneasy horse, hairline crack
in fresco wall]`

**20** — Lakin yaad ha na — in logon ka pass volcano ka lafz hi nahi tha? To
unho na wohi socha jo hum sab sochta: thora bohat hilna to yahan normal ha.
62 wala zalzala bhi to guzar hi gaya tha. Zindagi chalti rahi.
`[back to lively street — but now Vesuvius is ALWAYS in frame background.
Visual refrain locked]`

**21** — Or shayad isi liya, 24 October ki us subah, jab bread bik rahi thi or
bacha parh raha thay... kisi na pahar ki taraf dakha tak nahi.
`[long lens: busy street foreground, mountain soft-focus behind. Hold.
First faint rumble in sound design]`

**22** — Ruko. Ma na 24 October kaha? Ap na shayad hamesha 24 August suna ho
ga. Ya galti nahi ha — ya history ka ak bohat bara twist ha, or is ka saboot
2018 ma ja kar mila. Wo kahani akhir ma. Pehla — dopehar ho chuki ha.
`[open loop #2: calendar card flashes "24 AUG?" → glitches to "24 OCT?" →
locked with a "AKHIR MA" stamp. Timestamp chip: "12:00 PM"]`

### THE ERUPTION — 7:30–9:20 · dopehar

**23** — 12 or 1 baja ka darmiyan... pahar phat gaya.
`[SILENCE for a beat — then the blast. Full-screen eruption. This is the
pattern interrupt of the video]`

**24** — Ak dhamaka jo itna zordar tha ka pathar or aag ka aik satoon — yani
column — aasman ma 33 KILOMETER tak gaya. 33 kilometer ka matlab samajhta ho?
Jahan sa jahaaz urta hain, us sa 3 guna upar.
`[column rising, scale diagram card: airplane 10km ✈ / column 33km — parchment
infographic]`

**25** — 30 kilometer door, khaari ka doosri taraf, ak 17 saal ka larka ya sab
apni aankhon sa dakh raha tha. Us ka naam — Pliny. Us na baad ma likha ka
badal ki shakal cheer ka darakht jaisi thi — lamba tana, or upar ja kar
phailta hova shakhain.
`[map: Misenum ↔ Vesuvius across bay. AI recon: teenager on terrace watching.
His actual quote fades in as parchment text]`

**26** — Or yaad rakhna — jo kuch hum aaj is din ka bara ma janta hain na, us
ka aadha hissa is 17 saal ka larka ki wajah sa ha. Us na sab likh diya. History
ka pehla eyewitness report jo kisi volcano ka bara ma likhi gayi.
`[his letters — real manuscript imagery, evidence layer]`

**27** — Pompeii ma dopehar 1 baja din raat ma badal gaya. Suraj ghaib. Aasman
sa pathar girna shuru — chota, halka, jhaag jaisa pathar jis ko pumice kehta
hain. Pehla pehla to log hairan thay. Kuch na to isa tamasha samjha.
`[ash darkness falls over street; pumice starts pattering. People catching
stones, looking up]`

**28** — Ab yahan pa har insan ka samna wohi sawal tha jo shayad zindagi ka sab
sa mushkil sawal ha. Bhaago... ya chhupo?
`[freeze on a family at a doorway. Card: "BHAAGO ya CHHUPO?" — the decision
that decides everything]`

**29** — Jo log usi waqt nikal gaya — pehla 1-2 ghanta ma — un ma sa zyada tar
bach gaya. Ya bat scientists ab confirm kar chuka hain. Lakin hazaron log ruka.
Kisi ko apna maal samaitna tha. Kisi ka khayal tha ka toofan guzar jaya ga.
Kisi ka bachay chota thay, ya walidain boorha.
`[exodus on the road — crowds with bundles; then interiors: people sheltering,
barring doors]`

**30** — Or dakho — un pa hansna asaan ha. Lakin khud socho. Agar aaj ap ka
shehar pa pathar barasna lagain, or ap ko ya lafz "volcano" pata hi na ho...
to kya ap apna ghar chhor deta? Sach bataon? Shayad ma bhi ruk jata.
`[direct-address beat — slow dolly through a home, family in lamplight]`

### THE LONG AFTERNOON & NIGHT — 9:20–11:40

**31** — 2 baja. 3 baja. 4 baja. Pathar rukta nahi. Har ghanta koi 15
centimeter nayi teh jam rahi ha. Galiyan bharti ja rahi hain. Darwaza band
hona laga hain — bahar sa.
`[timelapse: street filling with grey pumice, cart wheels buried, door
half-buried. Timestamp chips ticking: 2PM · 3PM · 4PM]`

**32** — Or samandar ka rasta? Wo bhi band. Pumice pani pa tairta ha — poori
khaari patharon ka farsh ban gayi. Jahaz na andar aa sakta thay na bahar ja
sakta thay.
`[harbor choked with floating pumice — escape route #2 gone]`

**33** — Isi waqt khaari ka par ak admi na kuch aisa kiya jo history ma amar ho
gaya. Pliny ka chacha — Roman navy ka admiral — na apna behra ka rukh seedha
tabahi ki TARAF kar diya. Rescue mission. Us na kaha tha: "Qismat bahadur ka
sath hoti ha."
`[fleet cutting across the bay toward the black column — hero shot. Quote card]`

**34** — Wo hazaron logon ko bachana ki koshish ma khud wahan phans gaya. Agli
subah, samandar kinara pa, us ki maut ho gayi. Dunya ka pehla recorded
volcanologist... jo apna subject ko samajhna ki qeemat jaan sa da gaya.
`[beach at Stabiae, ash-heavy air, the admiral collapsed. Respectful, not gory]`

**35** — Sham 7 baja. Ab tak 6 ghanta pathar baras chuka ha. Or ab woh cheez
shuru hui jis na is tabahi ko history ki sab sa khatarnak tabahi banaya.
Chhatain girna lagi hain.
`[timestamp "7:00 PM". Interior: ceiling beam cracks under pumice weight —
first collapse. Mid-video tension zone opens]`

**36** — Roman chhatain tirchi hoti thin — lakri ka shahteeron pa bhaari tiled
khaprail. Or har square meter pa ab 100 kilo sa zyada wazan tha. Jo log gharon
ma chhupa thay na — jin ko lagta tha ka andar mehfooz hain — unhi ka upar ghar
girna laga.
`[parchment physics card: PITCHED tiled roof + weight arrows + collapse diagram —
"100+ KG / m²" (fact-fix: Roman roofs were pitched-tiled, NOT flat)]`

**37** — Or pahar? Pahar ka andar ab ak nayi cheez ho rahi thi. Wo 33 km wala
column apna hi wazan sa larkharana laga tha. Scientists is lamha ko kehta hain
"column collapse". Or jab wo girta ha na... to neecha ki taraf aag ka darya
banta ha. Naam yaad kar lo — pyroclastic flow. 500 degree garam gas or raakh,
100 kilometer per hour ki raftar sa.
`[CGI: column base destabilizing. Parchment card: "PYROCLASTIC FLOW = aag +
gas + raakh · ~500°C · 100+ km/h · bhaagna namumkin"]`

**38** — Raat ka andhera ma pehli lehrain aayin. Pehla nishana Pompeii nahi
tha. Herculaneum tha — pahar ka doosri taraf ka chota ameer shehar. Wahan jo
hova, wo ma ak hi jumla ma kahon ga. Jo log samandar kinara boat ka intezar
ma kharee thay... un ka liya sab kuch ak second sa bhi kam ma khatam ho gaya.
`[map: first surges hit Herculaneum. Then: the real boathouse ruins today,
silent, desaturated. No recreation of the deaths — the restraint IS the power]`

**39** — 2,000 saal baad jab scientists na wahan ki haddiyan dakhein, to unho
na hisaab lagaya — garmi itni thi ka maut ka ehsaas tak hona ka waqt nahi
mila. Ya shayad us poori raat ki wahid rehmat thi.
`[evidence layer: skeletal remains photos from boathouses — museum-style,
respectful framing]`

### DAWN — THE END — 11:40–14:40 · [the peak]

**40** — Pompeii ma raat kisi tarah guzri. 80 minute ka waqfa sa lehr pa lehr
aati rahi, lakin shehar ki deewaron or fasla ki wajah sa woh Pompeii tak poori

`[night vigil: lamplight interiors, families huddled, ash falling outside
windows. Timestamp: "3:00 AM"]`

**41** — Phir subah hona sa thora pehla... sab kuch ruk gaya. Pathar girna
band. Khamoshi.
`[dead silence — no music. Grey pre-dawn light on buried streets]`

**42** — Or yehi khamoshi sab sa bara dhoka thi.
`[hold the silence one beat longer]`

**43** — Logon na socha — bas, guzar gaya. Darwaza khula. Log galiyon ma nikla.
Kandhon pa bacha, haathon ma potliyan. Wo pathar ki 3 meter moti teh ka upar
chal kar shehar sa nikalna laga.
`[survivors emerging, climbing over the pumice layer — hope shot, warm light
crack on horizon. Timestamp: "6:30 AM"]`

**44** — Lakin scientists ab janta hain ka us lamha pahar ka andar kya ho raha
tha. Wo 32 ghanta ka is eruption ka akhri or sab sa bara war tayar kar raha
tha. Poora column... gir gaya.
`[the final collapse begins — vast, slow, inevitable]`

**45** — Jo lehr ab aayi, wo pichli sab sa bari thi. Ya wali shehar ki deewar
pa nahi ruki. Or jo log galiyon ma thay, gharon ma thay, bhaag raha thay...
Pompeii ma us subah koi 2,000 log baqi thay. Or phir... koi nahi tha.
`[the surge crests the wall — screen washes to white-grey. Then: total
stillness. Ash falling like snow. No screaming shown. Peak of the film]`

**46** — Jab sab khatam hova, to jahan shehar tha wahan sirf ak khamosh,
dhuan uthta hova maidan tha. 6 meter gehri raakh ka neecha — galiyan, ghar,
bakery ki 81 rotiyan, deewaron ka likha hova paigham, or hazaron log. Sab
waqt ma jam gaya. Theek us lamha pa jis lamha wo thay.
`[aerial pull-back: featureless grey plain where the city was. Vesuvius now
shorter, broken. Music: single held note]`

### 1700 SAAL — 14:40–16:00 · [rediscovery + casts]

**52** — Or yaad ha video ka shuru wala ghora? Bilkul haal ma, ak nayi khudai

Na ghore ko, na us ghar walon ko. Isi liya ma na shuru ma kaha tha — ya kahani
eruption ki nahi ha. In logon ki, in jaanon ki ha.
`[match frame: recon horse (0:08) ↔ the Civita Giuliana harnessed-horse cast.

Callback to seg 03.]`

**53** — Agar ya video ap ko ab tak rok ka rakha hui ha, to subscribe kar

`[soft CTA over excavation b-roll — placed right after the emotional payoff,

# Pompeii — master shot list (storyboard)

## Method (how each segment is broken into shots)

- **Shot length:** aim **3–5 s** per RECON still (a still + Ken Burns holds ~3–5 s before it feels
  dead). Detail inserts & match-cut pairs ~2–3 s. Deliberate **hold** beats (silence, peak, a hero
  shot) may run 6–10 s on one image on purpose.
- **Shots per segment** = round(VO seconds ÷ ~4 s), then adjusted by the number of distinct **visual
  beats** in the VO. Not hardcoded — if ONE image serves the whole line, one image; if the line moves
  (new subject/angle/moment), split it. Reveals and "2 quick cuts" lines = ≥2 shots.
- **Layer tags:** `[RECON]` = Nano Banana still (has a prompt) · `[CARD]` = parchment card built in
  HyperFrames (no image gen) · `[EVIDENCE]` = real ruin/cast/fresco footage, sourced (no image gen).
  **Only `[RECON]` costs an image generation.**
- **Motion** noted per shot (push-in / pull-back / tilt / hold / parallax) — see [[DESIGN]] motion=emotion.
- **Consistency:** do each SECTION in ONE Nano Banana chat; paste its SETUP block first (below), then
  the shots in order. Protocol + character locks: [[image-prompts]].

**The 10 sections (= the script's own acts), one per "continue":**
1. Hook (01–07) · 2. Setup (08–12) · 3. The Morning (13–22) · 4. The Eruption (23–30) ·
5. Afternoon & Night (31–39) · 6. Dawn/The End (40–46) · 7. 1700 Years / Casts (47–53) ·
8. 2024–26 Discoveries (54–61) · 9. Ibrah coda (61a–61d) · 10. Ending (62–65).

**Fact-check (verified 2026-07-12, this session):**
- Column **~33 km**, **umbrella-pine** shape — narrow ash trunk rising from the summit, spreading at the
  top into branching canopy; penetrated the stratosphere; grey-white/brown boiling convective cloud
  ("Plinian" is named for exactly this). [Wikipedia — Eruption of Vesuvius 79 AD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eruption_of_Mount_Vesuvius_in_79_AD) ·
  [Plinian eruption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plinian_eruption). Airplane cruise ~10 km → column ~3×.
- **Pliny the Younger = 17**, at **Misenum ~30 km** NW across the Bay of Naples, studying with his tutor
  when it began; described the cloud as a **pine tree** — "a very tall trunk, spreading out at the top into
  branches"; wrote it to Tacitus ~25 yrs later. [Pompeii Arch. Park — Through the Eyes of History](https://pompeiiarchaeologicalpark.com/through-the-eyes-of-history-pliny-the-youngers-account-of-vesuvius/) ·
  [Albert — Pliny Letter 6.16](https://www.albert.io/blog/pliny-letter-20-younger-pliny-vesuvius-ap-latin-review/).
- **Pumice/lapilli fall at Pompeii** (~8 km from crater): sky darkened within minutes, day→night; **small
  white frothy porous stones** fell like hail, ~15–20 cm/hr, piling in the streets; people **wrapped
  pillows/cloths over their heads** and fled through the gates. [pompeiicity — hour-by-hour](https://pompeiicity.com/en/history/eruption) ·
  [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/history/fall-rise-fall-pompeii-180955732/).

Here's the whole idea. Every dollar gets a job before the month starts. Fifty percent to needs, thirty to wants, twenty to savings and debt payoff. It comes from Elizabeth Warren and Amelia Warren Tyagi's book, All Your Worth. And here's what almost everyone gets wrong — it's fifty, thirty, twenty of your take-home pay. After taxes, after your four-oh-one-k. The number that lands in the account, not the one on the offer letter.

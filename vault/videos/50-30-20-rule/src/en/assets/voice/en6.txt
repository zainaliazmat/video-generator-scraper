The last twenty percent is the only slice that ever makes you richer. Emergency fund first, then retirement, then anything above the minimum on your debt. And that debt piece matters — the average cardholder carries about sixty-seven hundred dollars, and if you revolve a balance, closer to eleven thousand. At twenty-plus percent interest, killing that balance is a guaranteed return. Nothing in the market pays you twenty percent risk-free. Everybody cuts this line first. It's the only one that pays you back.

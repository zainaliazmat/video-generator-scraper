## Description

```
Every budgeting video starts with the 50/30/20 rule: half your take-home to needs, 30% to wants, 20% to savings. Nobody mentions that it was written in 2005 — and that median rent in America is now about $1,850 a month. On a $4,000 paycheck, that's almost the entire needs bucket, gone, before groceries.

The rule isn't wrong. It's dated. This is the whole thing in under four minutes: where the rule actually comes from, what belongs in each bucket, the real math on a $4,000 take-home ($2,000 / $1,200 / $800), and the 2026 patch — if your needs eat 65%, run 65/15/20. Cut the wants, not the savings. The 20 never moves.

One detail almost everyone gets wrong: it's 50/30/20 of your TAKE-HOME pay — after taxes, after your 401(k) — not the number on your offer letter.
```

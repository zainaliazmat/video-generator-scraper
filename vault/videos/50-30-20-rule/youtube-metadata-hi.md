**Two things this means.**

1. **Our angle is validated.** India's biggest personal-finance creator built a
   507k-view video on exactly our thesis — that 50-30-20 does not fit Indian
   incomes and needs a re-cut. We are not wrong; we are late.
2. **We cannot win "50 30 20 rule of money" head-on.** Warikoo's videos are the
   autocomplete (`50 30 20 rule of money warikoo` is itself a suggested query).
   Everything else on that keyword is sub-100-view AI slop — the term is
   saturated, not open.

**Our difference is real and worth stating in the video's favour:** Warikoo's
patch is **65-20-15** (needs 65 / wants 20 / **savings cut to 15**). Ours is
**65-15-20** (needs 65 / **wants cut to 15** / savings held at 20). We protect
the savings line; he protects the fun line. That is a genuine, defensible
disagreement — but do **not** put "65-20-15" in the title or tags, or we're
fighting his SEO with his own keyword.

**Therefore the title targets the salary-management cluster, not the rule name.**
That lane is healthy (487k / 256k / 217k across several channels, no single
owner) and matches our video's worked example.

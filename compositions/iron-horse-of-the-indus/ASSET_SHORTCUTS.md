# Asset Shortcuts — *The Iron Horse of the Indus*

Sourcing links for **you to download images yourself**. The Asset Scout does not download or embed
anything — these are search/landing URLs into real archives. Exact asset IDs drift, so most links are
**search queries or category pages** (preferred); follow them, pick the best frame, save under the
filename given.

---

## Licensing reminder (read first)

- **Prefer Public Domain / CC0.** Almost everything in this film is pre-1928 / British-Raj-era material,
  which is overwhelmingly **public domain** (author death + life-of-author terms long expired; UK Crown
  copyright on official photos expires 50 yrs after publication). Proceed confidently on the 19th-c. bridges,
  stations, and tunnels.
- **Skip** watermarked previews, "editorial use only" stock, Getty/Alamy **rights-managed** items, and any
  "all rights reserved" modern scan where a clean PD original exists elsewhere. A museum can charge for a
  *high-res scan* of a PD photo — the underlying image is still PD; look for the free/low-res or Commons copy first.
- **Always record source + license** for every file in **`CREDITS.txt`** (institution, collection/shelfmark or
  Commons file URL, photographer if known, license tag). Do this as you download — back-filling is misery.
- When two candidates tie, pick the one with the **clearest provenance and date**, not the prettiest crop.

## Ethics note for s22 (refugee trains) — read before sourcing

This beat is a human catastrophe, **not** spectacle. The script handles it with restraint ("we remember them
quietly, and let the empty platforms speak"). When sourcing:

1. **Choose the quiet, non-graphic frame.** A packed-but-calm carriage, a crowded platform, or — when in any
   doubt — an **empty platform** or a general-migration image. Never gore, never a "ghost train," never a
   named-massacre photo.
2. **Verify date and side.** Confirm the image is genuinely Aug–Sept 1947 and that the direction of travel
   (Muslims toward Pakistan / Hindus & Sikhs toward India) matches what you imply. Partition photos are
   *routinely* mis-dated and mis-sided — do not caption a generic crowded-train photo as a specific event.
3. **Verify attribution.** Use only images with credible provenance (Partition Museum / Google Arts & Culture,
   Science & Industry Museum, F.E. Chaudhry press archive, BL/IOR). Record the full credit in `CREDITS.txt`.
4. **When uncertain, default to restraint** — the emptier, quieter frame is always the safer and more dignified
   choice for this beat.

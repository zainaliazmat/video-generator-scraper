# Storyboard — The Iron Horse of the Indus

> Drives the Composition Engineer. The look is **LOCKED** by `DESIGN.md` (vintage blue-sepia B&W, Ken Burns parallax, ~1.0s cross-dissolve). This file only specifies **shot content + motion within that grammar**. Scene #, year, and title are authoritative from `docs/script.md`.

## Locked conventions (read once)

- **Ken Burns**: every photo scales 1.0 → **1.12–1.16**, sine.inOut, full scene. Direction **ALTERNATES** scene to scene: `dir +1, −1, +1, …`. Even scene index = **+1**, odd = **−1**. Push amount noted per scene.
- **Parallax**: text drifts ~25–35% of the photo's travel (engine kenBurns text drift) — depth, not motion. Photo is the near plane and moves more.
- **Transition IN**: default **1.0s cross-dissolve** everywhere, EXCEPT the three act-break blooms below.
- **Text-block side**: text is left-aligned + vertically centered, so every body photo is framed to leave the **left third clear** (negative space on the left; subject weight on the right). Title card (s00) and outro (s25) are CENTERED, no band.
- **Paper milestones** (s02 charter, s05 merger): document / map plate, **never a train**.

## ★ ACT-BREAK BLOOMS (confirmed) — soft white bloom on the IN of these three only:
- **s06 — Act I → II** (1878, the bridge-building era begins)
- **s14 — Act II → III** (1904, the system at its height)
- **s21 — Act III → IV** (1947, partition)

Every other scene IN = plain 1.0s cross-dissolve.

Before this video ends…
you'll unlock your phone
four more times.
You won't decide to.
You just will.
This is the dark truth
behind social media.
Five point six billion of us.
Two hours, twenty-one minutes a day.
Every single day.
Do the math —
that's thirty-six days a year.
A month and a week of your life, gone.
Vanished into the scroll.
Here's what no one says out loud:
this isn't a bug.
It's the product.
The endless feed.
The pull-to-refresh.
The little red dots.
Every one is a slot machine
for your brain.
Pull. Wait.
Maybe you win.
Pull again.
Don't take my word for it.
The man who invented infinite scroll
calls it “behavioral cocaine.”
The engineer who built pull-to-refresh
said, simply:
“These are not good things.”
They know exactly what they built.
And the cost isn't just time.
It's your attention,
sliced into confetti.
It's measuring your real life
against everyone else's highlight reel.
The feed profits when
you feel like you're not enough.
So stop blaming your willpower.
You're not weak —
you're outgunned.
In twenty twenty-six,
juries found Meta and Google negligent
for designing this to hook kids.
The companies are appealing.
But the verdict on the design?
It's in.
You can fight back.
And it's not deleting everything.
Kill the red badges.
Switch your screen to grayscale —
no color, no hook.
Pick two windows a day.
And let yourself be bored.
Boredom is where you come back.
The algorithm doesn't care
about your peace.
So protect it yourself.
Log off. Breathe.
Live real.
